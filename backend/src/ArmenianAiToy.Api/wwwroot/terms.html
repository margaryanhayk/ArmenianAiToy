Areg — Terms of Service

Version 1.0 · Last updated 1 August 2026

The short version. Areg is an Armenian-speaking storytelling toy for children of about 4–7. A parent creates the account and stays in charge of it. Areg is free while we are testing, it is an early version that can break, and it is not a safety device — never rely on it in an emergency. You can delete your data or your account at any time.

1. What Areg is

Areg is a physical toy with an Armenian-speaking voice, together with a parent dashboard and a parent app. It tells stories, plays games, asks riddles, answers short curiosity questions and does calm bedtime talk.

Areg is deliberately not an open-ended chatbot, not a friend, not a teacher, and not a therapist. It will not hold an emotional-companion conversation, and it is not designed to replace adult attention.

2. Who may use it

  • You must be at least 18 and the parent or legal guardian of the child who will use the toy.
  • By creating an account you confirm you consent, as that child's parent or guardian, to the toy recording and processing what your child says, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • If another family's child will use your toy, you must have that child's parent's consent first.

3. Your account

  • Keep your password to yourself. Anything done with your account is treated as done by you.
  • Give a real email address — it is how you recover the account and how we reach you.
  • Tell us promptly if you think someone else has got into your account. You can revoke a toy's access yourself at any time.

4. Using it properly

Please do not:

  • try to break, overload, probe or reverse-engineer the service;
  • use it to generate harmful, illegal or abusive content;
  • use another person's account, or let a child use the parent dashboard;
  • resell the service or present it as your own product.

5. Safety — what we do and what we cannot promise

  • Every message from your child is checked automatically before Areg answers, and Areg's answer is checked again before your child hears it.
  • Conversation is limited to the five kinds of play listed above.
  • You can read every word, pause the toy instantly, set bedtime quiet hours, switch off individual play modes, or cut the toy off entirely.

Automatic safety checks are not perfect. Areg is powered by an AI language model. It can occasionally say something inaccurate, odd, or not what you would have chosen. It is meant to be used with a parent's knowledge, not as unsupervised childcare. Please read the conversations, and tell us if you see something wrong.

Areg is not an emergency device. It cannot call for help, cannot contact anyone, and must never be relied on for the safety, medical care or supervision of a child.

This version of Areg is being tested with a small number of families. That honestly means:

  • it may be unavailable, slow, or broken at times, without notice;
  • features may change or be removed;
  • although we take care not to, data could be lost during this phase;
  • it is not a certified retail toy and is not sold in shops.

7. Content and stories

  • Stories, games and riddles are generated by an AI model, or are adapted from classic Armenian literary works.
  • You may enjoy them with your family. Please do not republish or sell them as your own.
  • What your child says stays yours. We use it only to run and protect the service, as set out in the Privacy Policy.
