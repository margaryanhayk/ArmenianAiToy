Areg

An Armenian storytelling toy

Stories, games and riddles — in Armenian.

Areg is a soft toy that tells your child a story, plays a game, asks a riddle, and answers the odd question — all in Armenian. One button, one voice. No screen.

Open the parent dashboard →
Sign in, or create an account. If you have a toy, you can add it there with the code from its box.
For parents
👀 You see what your child heard Every conversation, every story played, and the answers your child gave after each one.
🛡️ Everything is checked before your child hears it Both what your child says and what Areg answers are checked. If something is held back, you are told.
🌙 You set the rules Quiet hours, which kinds of play are allowed, and a pause button that stops the toy at once.
🤐 Not a chatbot Areg is a play leader and a storyteller. It does not do open-ended conversation, and it is not your child's friend.
