Areg — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 1 August 2026

The short version.

Areg is an Armenian-speaking storytelling toy for children aged about 4–7. To answer your child, the toy sends what your child says to our server, and our server sends it to OpenAI to turn speech into text, write the reply, and speak it back.

We keep what was said so that you can read it. Conversations and voice recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can read, download or delete everything at any time, and deleting your account erases your family's data.

We do not sell data, we do not advertise, and there are no trackers or analytics in the app or on the toy.

1. Who is responsible

Areg is operated by Hayk Margaryan, an individual based in Armenia. If you have a question, a request, or a complaint about your data, write to haykmargaryan97@gmail.com.

Areg is currently in an early testing phase with a small number of families. Please read section 11, which is honest about what that means.

2. Who this policy is for

This policy is written for the parent or legal guardian. Only an adult can create an Areg account. A child never has an account, never signs in, and cannot change any setting. Everything about the child is entered and controlled by you.

3. What we collect

About you (the parent)

  • Your email address.
  • Your password — stored only as an unreadable cryptographic hash (bcrypt). We never see or store the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: the account identifier Google gives us, and your email address. We never receive your Google password.
  • When you registered, when you last signed in, whether you confirmed your email address, and which version of the terms you accepted.

About your child

  • First name — so Areg can address your child by name.
  • Year of birth or age — so stories suit the right age.
  • Gender — Armenian grammar changes with it; this is the only reason it is asked.
  • Which of the five play modes you allow for that child.

We do not ask for a surname, a photo, a school, an address, a phone number, or a date of birth beyond the year.

About the toy

  • Its identifier and hardware address, the name you give it, and its firmware version.
  • When it was last online, and its time zone (used for bedtime).
  • The settings you choose: paused, bedtime hours, which modes are on.

What is said

  • The text of what your child says and what Areg answers, with the time of each message.
  • The voice recording of what your child said, and the audio of Areg's reply.
  • A safety label on each message, from the automatic safety check.

Account activity

A log of sensitive actions on your own account — password changed, toy unlinked, conversation deleted, data exported, and so on. It records the action and the time, never the content of a conversation.

What we do not collect

  • No location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone access except when your child presses the toy's button.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs in the parent app or on the toy.
  • No payment details — Areg is free during the testing phase.

5. Who else sees the data

We use three companies to run the service. They act on our instructions and may not use your family's data for their own purposes.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or give it to anyone else — except if we are legally required to by a valid order from a competent authority.

6. Where the data is kept

On servers in the United States (our hosting provider's US region). Traffic between the app, the toy and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). If you are in Armenia or the EU, this means your data is transferred to the United States.

8. Your rights and your controls

You do not have to ask us — these are buttons in the parent dashboard and the app:

  • See everything — every conversation, word for word, and every safety flag.
  • Listen — play back what Areg said.
  • Download everything — one file with all of your family's data (Export).
  • Delete — a single conversation, a child, a toy, or your entire account. Deleting the account erases your family's data.
  • Stop the toy — pause it instantly, set bedtime quiet hours, switch off any of the play modes, or revoke the toy's access entirely.

You also have the right to correct wrong information, to object to how we use the data, and to complain to your national data-protection authority.

9. Children

Areg is made for children of about 4–7 and is meant to be used with a parent's knowledge. The account, the consent and every control belong to the parent. Areg is a storyteller and play leader — it is deliberately not an open-ended chatbot, not a friend, and not an emotional companion. Conversation is limited to five kinds of play: stories, games, riddles, short curiosity answers, and calm bedtime talk.

Every message is checked automatically for unsafe content before Areg answers, and Areg's answer is checked again before your child hears it. Automatic checks are good but not perfect, which is why you can read everything.

10. Security

  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; the toy's secret key is stored hashed too.
  • Every request from the app is checked against your own account — one parent can never see another family's data.
  • A lost or stolen toy can be revoked instantly, which kills its access.
  • Repeated wrong passwords temporarily lock sign-in for that account.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you by email.

11. Honest limits of this version

  • Areg is in an early testing phase with a small number of families, not a finished retail product.
  • The export includes every conversation as text and tells you which messages have audio, but the child's own voice recordings cannot yet be downloaded individually. They are stored, they are deleted on schedule and on request, but there is no per-recording download button yet.
  • This document has been written carefully and truthfully by the operator, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.
