## The plan in one paragraph

**Stage 0** is the next two weeks. You fix the production settings, rotate the leaked keys, make the repo private, set up alerts and backups, and send the OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Google and lawyer letters, because their answers take weeks. **Stage 1a** gets the toy ready for your own child:
- one safety check on every path a child's words take
- guards on every model reply
- spoken "stop", "goodbye" and "that's dangerous"
- a grown-up answer to grief, fear and disclosures
- the chat model chosen from a measured bake-off
- firmware 1.3.5 proven on the bench and by ear, in a closed box

It ends with one supervised session with your child, realistically **1–15 December 2026**. **Stage 1b** makes data, accounts and parent visibility safe for other families. **Stage 2** delivers Wi-Fi setup, the app, the dashboard, ops and firmware 1.3.6. ## How we prove it worked

1. **The regression corpus** (`regression-corpus`):
   - About 280 cases in one versioned corpus: the existing 55, about 95 new ones, and about 130 sensitive-topic probes. Every case states the behaviour it expects.
   - The gate:
     - fails on any critical miss
     - needs ≥95 % of enforced cases to pass, and ≥90 % of harmless cases to get a normal answer
     - calls a run INVALID when the provider errors on more than 5 % of turns
   - Each safety package flips its own "tracking" cases to "enforced" in the same PR.
   - A model change must pass `requalify.sh` again. The System tab warns when the live model was never qualified.
2. **Re-run of both reviews' live tests.**
   - Each safety and ChatService package re-runs its own probes from the reviews before merging: the self-harm WAVs, P01–P25, R1–R6, the two persona transcripts, the second-owner walk, the 5,760-character name, and the bad-media uploads.
   - The **final re-run** inside `pilot-go-live` [critic] uses `requalify.sh --suite full --passes 5` on the deployed build and your chosen provider, plus voice paths and both persona replays. It maps every review id with a live probe to PASS or FAIL and shows you old and new Armenian replies side by side (≈45 min).
3. **Benchmarks.**
   - The `model-bakeoff` harness PR makes the Story, Game, Riddle, Calm, Curiosity and Mode benchmarks claim their toy and fail loudly when they measure nothing.
   - Baselines are written from your chosen model.
   - Every ChatService PR re-runs them in the fixed order [critic].
   - `story-gen-quality` adds before/after targets: repetition, length, the followed option, register.
4. **Automatic checks** (`ci-gates`):
   - `dotnet test`, starting from 3197; the count in CLAUDE.md is kept true and no test is ever skipped
   - firmware host tests and the release-image gate
   - firmware wire-contract tests and migration upgrade tests
   - sha/size tests for all 240 content files
   - secret scan and package-advisory gate
   - a Production boot smoke and the restore drill
   - Playwright smoke of parent.html and admin.html
5. **Bench and listen gates.**
   - `ht-bench-135`: 1.3.5 proven on a toy.
   - `ht-listen-loudness`: every file a child hears has a verdict pinned to its sha256, the toy's maximum loudness is measured, and a native listener signs.
   - `ht-first-child`: a real child, with transcript errors and cost measured.
   - `ht-pilot-image`: Wi-Fi setup on Android and iPhone, OTA, outages, soak.
   - `ht-reva-lab`: rev-A power, battery and EMC.
   - Any change to an audio file's bytes means a new listen before it ships.
