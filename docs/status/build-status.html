The shortest path to five real families

  1. 1

    Record all ten stories in the voice you picked — the three broken ones made whole in the same pass.

    This is the one that changes how the toy feels, and it is the cheapest step on this page. Two of the ten have never been recorded at all — they are twenty seconds each. The forest and river sounds are not in this pass: no sound has been bought yet, and they do not change whether the toy works. They ride the next recording.

  2. 2

    Wire the two buttons and test everything on your own toy for a week — games, pauses, greeting.

    Your own children are the honest test. Anything they ignore, we cut.

  3. 3

    Build four more toys by hand on the same boards you already use, in simple cases.

    Do not wait for the factory board for this. Five hand-made toys are enough to learn everything a first version can teach.

  4. 4

    Give them to five families you know, free, for a month.

    Before this step, have someone check what you legally owe families in Armenia and the EU even when giving a device away — I can prepare the questions, but a lawyer answers them.

  5. 5

    Then decide about the factory board, the case, certification and selling.

    Those cost thousands. Spend them after five families tell you the toy is worth it, not before.
