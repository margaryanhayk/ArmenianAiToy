Ask the storyteller one question: would you allow an AI voice to be made from your voice?

Ask it before money is discussed. Some performers refuse at any price, and it is better to know in the first conversation. Everything else in the project is waiting behind his answer. Ask what he would charge to record the ten stories too, in case he says no to the AI part.

Waiting on you

Only three things in the whole project need you. Everything else is mine.

  • Ask

    The storyteller — will he allow an AI voice?

    You decided: a real, famous Armenian storyteller, paid, with an AI copy of his voice so you can add stories later yourself. That can be done — just not on ElevenLabs, which forbids copying another person even with their permission. An Armenian company in Yerevan, VS.AM, sells exactly this. But none of it starts until he says yes.

  • Read

    The Armenian texts, and the 29 sound moments.

    158 lines are waiting on your phone — the quiz, the Vardan and Katrin game, the after-story questions, the Tsivik serial. New this week: 29 places where a forest, a river or a door would come into a story. Answer both with numbers. You can do this today; it does not wait for the voice.

  • Buy

    Two push buttons.

    About 500 dram at the Yerevan radio shop. Without them the three games inside the toy cannot be tested at all.

One thing to remember much later, when you book the studio: ask for one sound file per part of a story, not one file for the whole story. It costs the studio nothing, it lets the toy know exactly where the child is in the tale, and it cannot be added after the recording is finished.

The shortest path to five real families

  1. 1

    Settle the storyteller, then record all ten stories once — new voice, the three broken ones made whole, and the forest and river sounds mixed in.

    This is the one that changes how the toy feels. Three problems solved in a single session instead of three. Two of the ten stories have never been recorded at all and are twenty seconds each — while he is in the room they cost a minute.

  2. 2

    Wire the two buttons and test everything on your own toy for a week — games, pauses, greeting.

    Your own children are the honest test. Anything they ignore, we cut.

  3. 3

    Build four more toys by hand on the same boards you already use, in simple cases.

    Do not wait for the factory board for this. Five hand-made toys are enough to learn everything a first version can teach.

  4. 4

    Give them to five families you know, free, for a month.

    Before this step, have someone check what you legally owe families in Armenia and the EU even when giving a device away — I can prepare the questions, but a lawyer answers them.

  5. 5

    Then decide about the factory board, the case, certification and selling.

    Those cost thousands. Spend them after five families tell you the toy is worth it, not before.
