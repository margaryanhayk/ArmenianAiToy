Three things to know before the evening

  • Decide

    The repository is public, and two real credentials are still in its history.

    Wi-Fi and device credentials in config.h across four commits in June, and a real device key inside the firmware image committed on 13 August and reverted the same day. Nothing in the repo records that the device was revoked or the Wi-Fi password changed. Two honest options for B4: accept it (revoke and re-provision that device in the console, change the home Wi-Fi password, reword B4 to "every committed secret is revoked, a full history scan is recorded, a scanner gates commits"), or rewrite history (filter-repo, force-push, re-clone every machine, ask GitHub to purge).
