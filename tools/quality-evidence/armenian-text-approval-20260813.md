## What this does NOT approve

**Nothing has been rendered, and no money has been spent on this.** Text
approval is one gate; the audio is another.

- **The 92 game clips already on the SD card are a bench-only working
  library.** The owner's standing caveat is that every one gets an expressive
  re-render — acting, not merely correct pronunciation — plus a fresh listen
  test, before launch. Approving the text is what unblocks that; it does not
  replace it.
- **The endings and the serial have never been rendered at all.**
- **The serial's approval is blanket, and the file says so.** Unlike the
  variant endings — where he rewrote seven of ten himself on 2026-08-07 — the
  six episodes had not been reviewed line by line before this. He read them on
  the page and approved; that is a real decision and it is recorded as what it
  is, so a later reader does not mistake it for the same depth of review the
  endings had.
- **The sample-first rule stands for the serial**: render the refrain, then
  episode one, then the closing clip, and verify «Ծիվի՛կ» before batching. It
  is the series' most-heard word and one bad pattern would poison all six.
